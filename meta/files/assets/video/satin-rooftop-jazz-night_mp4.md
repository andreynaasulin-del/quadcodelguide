- Text-to-video, not image-to-video. The anchor frame `assets/image/satin-rooftop-ref.png`

  IMAGE-INPUT and succeeds without it (`.temp/probe/satin-probe.mp4`). So the wardrobe and
  location have to be re-specified in words, verbatim from the frame that did pass.
- The wardrobe wording is copied unchanged from the version that cleared OpenAI's
  output-stage sexual-content filter: covered long-sleeve gown, high neckline, "fully
  clothed", "composed and dignified posture". Do not reintroduce "slip dress", "thin straps"
  or "side slit" — that is exactly what got the first anchor rejected.
- Night is written as an absence, not an adjective: "no daylight left on the horizon, no blue
  hour". Asking for "night" alone gets blue hour back, because the model has seen ten
  thousand rooftop blue-hour fashion plates.
- All the light on the subjects is now practical and warm — bulb string, candles in glass,
  one soft key. That is what buys "romantic" instead of "cold rooftop at night", and it gives
  the crystal heels a warm sparkle source in Beat 3 rather than nothing to catch.
- Live playing is specified mechanically (fingers on keys, breath in the chest, correct hand
  placement) because "a man playing saxophone" reliably renders as a man holding a saxophone.
  Same lesson as the thermal beat on the Tokyo film: name the mechanism, not the vibe.
- Beats are 3.0 / 3.5 / 3.0 / 3.5s. Two people in 13s needs the two-shot at the end to pay
  off the pairing; cutting faster would leave the musician as a prop.
- Explicit ban on mic stands, amps, cables and music stands. Ask for "live music on a
  rooftop" and the model furnishes a stage.
- If Seedance's native audio comes back thin or the sax reads synthetic, replace with a Suno
  instrumental (solo tenor sax ballad, 70 BPM, MAKE_INSTRUMENTAL) and mux via
  `add_audio ... replace=true` — the route used for `guidebookside_music.mp4`.
