COMMENTS: ## Audio notes
- Third attempt. The first two failed by over-specifying: a Timbaland-style prompt
  gave smeared hand percussion, and a bar-by-bar boom-bap arrangement made Suno
  cram five section changes into a 30 s window. This prompt names a genre, four
  instruments and a tempo, and stops there.
- Non-custom mode on purpose — Suno's own genre priors for lo-fi are strong and
  well-trained, and fighting them with a custom STYLE block made things worse.
- 85 BPM, 2.82 s per bar. The 30.72 s video cut lands mid-bar, so the final trim
  gets a 1.5 s fade-out rather than a hard cut.
- Warm low end comes from the upright bass, which is the one instrument request
  carried over from the rejected takes.
