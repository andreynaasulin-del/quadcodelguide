  CHARACTER ARCHETYPE (shared DNA across all three):
  Extremely thin adult humanoid court diplomat.
  Very tall vertical proportion, narrow shoulders, long limbs, small head relative to height.
  Still, controlled posture — not combat-ready, not heroic wide stance.
  Hands often concealed or close to the body.
  Gothic / vampiric court presence through costume massing only, not fangs or bat wings as anatomy.

  VARIANT A — WING COLLAR:
  Pure black silhouette.
  Dominant mass: extremely high stiffened collar that flares outward and upward like folded bat wings behind the head, creating a wide top wedge over a needle-thin body.
  Long closed coat to the ankles, sleeves slightly too long, hiding the hands.
  Minimal base flare at the hem.
  One thin vertical cane or staff held close to the leg so it reads as a second vertical line.
  Pose: three-quarter idle, slight forward lean of the torso, feet close together.

  VARIANT B — RELIC CHAIN:
  Pure black silhouette.
  Dominant mass: heavy layered chain/reliquary cluster hanging from the neck and chest, breaking the thin torso with irregular chunky negative-space cuts.
  Shorter structured jacket ending mid-thigh, tight vertical legs, high boots.
  One arm bent holding a small rectangular case at the hip.
  Collar is present but lower and sharper than A — a knife-edge upright collar, not a wing flare.
  Pose: frontal-neutral, chin slightly raised, feet parallel.

  VARIANT C — MOTH CLOAK:
  Pure black silhouette.
  Dominant mass: long triangular cloak/mantle with ragged lower edge and high shoulder peaks, reading like folded moth wings when closed.
  Body mostly swallowed by the cloak; only lower legs and pointed footwear emerge.
  Hood up, head a smooth dark oval, no facial interior.
  One hand slightly extended forward from the cloak slit as an invitation gesture.
  Pose: three-quarter, head tilted, more triangular overall shape than A or B.

  SILHOUETTE RULES:
  All three figures pure solid black (#0B0C0B), no interior lines, no eyes, no mouth, no fabric texture, no jewelry sparkle, no gradient, no glow.
  Edge clarity only — every costume landmark must read from the outer contour alone.
  Same figure height across panels, aligned to a shared ground baseline.
  Generous spacing between figures so contours do not merge.
  Studio orthographic presentation, even lighting on the paper field, no environment, no cast drama beyond a faint soft contact shadow under each foot if needed.

  HARD CONSTRAINTS:
  Original design only. Do not resemble Loki, Dracula, Alucard, Sephiroth, or any copyrighted character.
  No color on the characters. No blood red. No gold. No UI chrome. No stats. No watermarks. No logos.
  No photoreal skin, no face detail, no multi-character crowd, no weapons larger than a thin cane/case.
  Keep labels legible and sparse. This is a production tool, not a poster.
