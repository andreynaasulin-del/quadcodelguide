PROMPT: |
  Wide 3:2 landscape cinematic photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, practical lighting only. Deliverable: an instantly recognizable Gamedev category banner — must read as "game character creation" in under a second, premium execution, not a generic stock photo, absolutely no cyberpunk/neon aesthetic.
  Scene: an early-20th-century industrial factory assembly line (think 1913 Ford Highland Park plant — riveted steel beams, wooden crate pallets, hanging incandescent bulbs on cloth-wrapped cords, a chain-driven overhead conveyor rail), but instead of car chassis, the line is building a fantasy RPG warrior. A humanoid figure made of glowing polygonal wireframe mesh over a half-finished bronze/steel armor shell hangs from the conveyor hook, mid-assembly: one gauntlet already fitted and detailed, the other arm still bare glowing wireframe. Sparks fly from a riveting tool where a factory worker (silhouette, period flat cap and apron, back to camera) is fastening a pauldron onto the shoulder. A small chest-height arcade cabinet sits bolted to a wooden workbench in the foreground, its CRT screen glowing with a simple 8-bit health-bar/stats HUD, warm phosphor green-amber glow, feeding a cable up into the character's chest like a diagnostic monitor.
  Lighting: warm tungsten factory light from the hanging bulbs and the crate window above, throwing long soft shadows; the wireframe portions of the character glow with a cool low-saturation cyan-white light strictly localized to the mesh itself (not a neon environment wash); the CRT arcade screen adds one small warm-green practical glow in the foreground. Deep soft shadow falling into the background at frame edges.
  Framing: character and conveyor hook dominant and centered, generous darker negative space in the upper-right and lower corners for a category label to sit on top later.
  Hard constraints: no text, no logos, no watermarks, no readable brand names, no purple/blue neon fog, no glitch/scanline overlay, no futuristic cyberpunk cityscape — this must read as a real period-industrial photograph with one contained sci-fi/fantasy element (the wireframe body + CRT), not a neon digital world.

DESCRIPTION: Category identity banner for the Gamedev section of Quadcode Guide — an RPG character under construction on an early-1900s factory assembly line, half wireframe mesh / half finished armor, a small arcade CRT feeding its build progress. Reads as "building a game character" instantly through a literal, human-scale industrial metaphor instead of an abstract or cyberpunk one.
USAGE: Gamedev category hero banner (section header background/cover) on quadcodeguide.vercel.app — candidate for the 16:10 card-cover crop used across category headers and listing cards. Not to be uploaded to production until explicit approval.
