# EMBER/01 — Modular Cooking Grid

## Goal
Create a senior-level industrial design and digital product case for a compact modular cooking system aimed at renters and small urban kitchens. Use GPT-Image only for visual assets. Every image must preserve one approved physical architecture.

## Product position
EMBER/01 replaces several countertop appliances with one 600 × 300 mm powered base and task-specific washable modules. The business model is a starter base plus recurring module and kit sales.

## Target user
- One- or two-person households
- Renters with limited counter and storage space
- People who cook often but cannot install a permanent hob
- Users who want to replace one damaged function without discarding a complete appliance

## Locked physical system
- Base: 600 × 300 × 28 mm
- Grid: four 150 × 300 mm module bays
- Electrical bus: sealed low-profile rear power rail with per-bay detection
- Locking: front mechanical latch plus rear guided power tongue; module cannot energize until both are seated
- Thermal rule: hot surfaces remain inside a 12 mm insulated perimeter; adjacent module walls use a 20 mm air channel
- Cleaning rule: all food-contact modules detach; powered base is wipe-clean only
- Maximum simultaneous load: 3.6 kW with automatic power allocation

## Module family
1. INDUCTION — 300 × 300 mm, two bays
2. GRILL — 300 × 300 mm, two bays, removable ribbed plate and grease cassette
3. DEEP PAN — 300 × 300 mm, two bays, lidded sauté and braise vessel
4. KETTLE DOCK — 150 × 300 mm, one bay
5. WARM TILE — 150 × 300 mm, one bay

## Visual direction
- Character: compact professional equipment, calm domestic presence
- Form: low rectangular base, precise radii, modules aligned to one strict grid
- Materials: graphite ceramic glass, bead-blasted recycled aluminium, charcoal silicone, warm off-white enamel
- Signal color: restrained ember orange used only for heat and lock status
- Typography: neutral grotesk, tabular numerals, sparse English labels
- Avoid: luxury-kitchen marble, sci-fi lighting, glossy black plastic, decorative screens, impossible floating parts

## Consistency gates
- Base remains 600 × 300 mm and the bay grid remains 150 × 300 mm
- Every module must occupy one or two whole bays
- Rear power tongue and front latch appear in every technical view
- No module energizes without a visible dual-lock logic
- Food-contact components are removable; base never goes under water
- Product-bearing generations use the approved architecture board or master CGI as first reference
- English text only, rendered verbatim and kept sparse
- Reject any image with changed module proportions or invented controls
