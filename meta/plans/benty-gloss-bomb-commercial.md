# Benty Beauty Gloss Bomb — 15s VEO Commercial

## Goal
Create a 15-second 16:9 luxury beauty commercial from three independent 5-second VEO 3 clips, then add exact typography and one continuous English voiceover in post.

## Production logic
- VEO clips remain text-free because generated typography is unreliable.
- Use three clips rather than one overloaded prompt: product macro, model montage, final editorial/product hero.
- Preserve a consistent translucent blush-pink cylindrical gloss tube with silver cap and shimmering pink formula.
- The fictional on-pack brand is BENTY BEAUTY, not Fenty Beauty.

## Steps
- [x] Review VEO 3 template and constraints: 5/10 seconds, 1280x720, prompt under 512 characters.
- [ ] Generate three 5-second VEO 3 clips in parallel.
- [ ] Inspect product geometry, lips, faces, hands and scene continuity.
- [ ] Concatenate clips into an exact 15-second silent master.
- [ ] Add exact on-screen text: “BENTY BEAUTY” and “Shine Without Limits.”
- [ ] Generate one continuous low, calm English voiceover: “One swipe. Endless shine. Gloss that steals every spotlight. Benty Beauty Gloss Bomb.”
- [ ] Mix restrained beauty-commercial Foley and voiceover.
- [ ] Export 1920x1080 H.264 MP4 at 30 FPS and inspect eight timeline points.
