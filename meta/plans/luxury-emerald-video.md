# Luxury Emerald Commercial Video Plan

## Goal
Turn the approved eight-frame storyboard into one 15-second 16:9 commercial while preserving the necklace, model, black-and-gold campaign language, and shot order. Seedance is blocked; Wan is explicitly rejected.

## Steps
- [x] Review all viable non-Wan video templates: Kling, Veo 3, Sora 2, Runway Gen-3, Grok, HappyHorse and Gemini Omni.
- [x] Crop and review eight clean cinematic references from the approved storyboard.
- [ ] Generate eight independent 5-second Kling Pro image-to-video shots. Independent shots avoid ugly morphs between unrelated storyboard scenes.
- [ ] Select the strongest 1.875 seconds from each shot, concatenate in order and normalize to exactly 15 seconds.
- [ ] Remove audio and deliver 1920 × 1080 H.264 at 30 FPS.
- [ ] Inspect eight timeline points for scene order, jewelry identity, model continuity, hands and generated artifacts.
- [ ] Deliver the MP4 or report the exact generation failure.

## Model decision
Kling Pro is the best fit here: strong product and human image animation, exact single-frame anchoring, and no need to morph between unrelated scenes. Veo 3 and Sora require extra spending confirmation and are restrictive with faces; HappyHorse is optimized for animals; Grok is limited to 720p; Gemini Omni accepts only seven references; Runway is viable but weaker than Kling for this jewelry-and-model sequence.
