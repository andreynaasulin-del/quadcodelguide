# SAVOR/01 — The Evidence of Food

## Goal
Build a senior motion-identity case from six GPT-Image keyframes and a production animation plan. The system turns scattered food evidence into four plain-language priorities without claiming to detect freshness or safety.

## Core idea
Food does not need another fake score. It needs its evidence organized. Receipts, printed dates, opened dates, storage zones and quantity signals enter as fragments, collide, then resolve into USE FIRST / USE SOON / STABLE / CHECK.

## Audience
Graphic designers, motion designers, product teams and households who need to understand the logic in under 20 seconds.

## Locked visual system
- Canvas: 1920 × 1080, 16:9, crop-safe for 9:16 and 1:1
- Grid: 12 columns, 64 px outer margin, 24 px gutters
- Palette: warm paper #F2EEE5, ink #20201E, tomato #C6382C, amber #C68A24, cobalt #1755A5, cool grey #9A9B96
- Type character: condensed grotesk display with neutral grotesk metadata and tabular numerals
- Material vocabulary: thermal receipt paper, date stamps, barcode stripes, food-label fragments, registration marks, halftone food photography
- Status coding: text + shape + color; color never carries meaning alone
- English only

## Six keyframes
1. Evidence Spill — uncontrolled fragments enter the frame
2. Date Collision — conflicting date language breaks the grid
3. Evidence Capture — metadata is isolated and registered
4. Triage Build — 12 items reorganize into priority order
5. Four States — the complete typographic status system appears
6. Final Lockup — SAVOR/01 / THE EVIDENCE OF FOOD

## Animation structure — 20 seconds
- 00:00–00:03 Evidence Spill
- 00:03–00:06 Date Collision
- 00:06–00:09 Evidence Capture
- 00:09–00:13 Triage Build
- 00:13–00:17 Four States
- 00:17–00:20 Final Lockup

## Motion rules
- Evidence enters with physical inertia, not generic easing
- OCR boxes snap only after a two-frame overshoot
- Barcode wipes drive cuts and masks
- Dates use vertical mechanical-counter motion
- Status changes happen by re-sorting position, then color; never color alone
- Camera remains orthographic; depth comes from paper layers and macro crops
- Maximum two simultaneous focal actions
- No morphing food, liquid simulation, holograms or fake analysis beams

## Sound direction
Dry scanner ticks, paper feed, refrigerator hum, date-stamp impacts and one low tonal pulse at final lockup. No corporate music bed. Sound accents follow evidence capture, not every cut.

## Safety rule
LOCKED — see [#rules.savor01-safety-rule]. SAVOR/01 organizes known information. It never claims to analyze food, never outputs a score, and never uses medical or danger iconography. Every status must be readable as text + shape + color, so meaning never depends on color alone.

## Production animation specification

### 00:00–00:03 — Evidence Spill
- Start on blank paper for 4 frames
- Receipt fragments enter from frame edges at 900–1400 px/s with 8% rotational overshoot
- Barcode wipes reveal halftone food fragments
- SAVOR/01 lockup stays still at center while evidence moves around it
- Sound: paper feed, two scanner ticks, low refrigerator hum

### 00:03–00:06 — Date Collision
- Match-cut the 21 JUN fragment from frame 01 into the giant black date
- USE BY slides left-to-right; OPENED counter-rolls downward; BEST BEFORE rotates 7° into collision
- Hold collision for 8 frames, then barcode shutter closes
- Sound: three dry stamp impacts, no whoosh

### 00:06–00:09 — Evidence Capture
- Barcode shutter opens onto the yoghurt crop
- Six registration boxes draw on clockwise over 36 frames
- Each box overshoots its target by 6 px for 2 frames, then snaps back
- NO SAFETY VERDICT appears last and remains neutral black
- Sound: six restrained optical clicks, one muted confirmation tone

### 00:09–00:13 — Triage Build
- Exactly 12 evidence tiles enter from scattered positions
- Tiles sort by vertical position first; status color appears 4 frames later
- Three tiles settle per lane with 3-frame stagger
- No tile scales or morphs; movement follows 65% ease-out with short physical recoil
- Sound: paper slides, four lane-lock impacts

### 00:13–00:17 — Four States
- Four vertical fields grow from hairline separators
- Status words reveal bottom-to-top through hard masks
- Circle, triangle, equals and X stamp in at 6-frame intervals
- CHECK uses the corrected diagonal X, never a medical plus
- Sound: four print-cylinder hits tuned from low to high

### 00:17–00:20 — Final Lockup
- Fields collapse into one horizontal receipt strip
- Barcode travels to center and stops dead
- Four symbols reorder to circle / triangle / equals / X
- SAVOR/01 resolves with a 2-frame red misregistration before alignment
- Hold final frame for 36 frames
- Sound: paper stop, low tonal pulse, refrigerator hum cuts on final frame

## Technical delivery
- Master: 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 20 seconds, ProRes 422 HQ
- Web: H.264, 16–20 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz
- Vertical: 1080 × 1920 using the protected center; reposition edge fragments, do not auto-crop
- Square: 1080 × 1080; retain status names and final lockup above 96 px safe margin

- Accessibility: minimum 4.5:1 text contrast; never communicate status by color alone
