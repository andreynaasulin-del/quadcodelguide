# Building Autopsy — canonical geometry and program

## Status
This file overrides earlier drafts. All subsequent drawings must follow it.

## Existing building
- Fictional 1912 textile factory, concept study only.
- Rectangle: 54.0 m east–west × 24.0 m north–south.
- Grid: seven numbered grid lines create six 9.0 m bays; five lettered grid lines create four 6.0 m bays.
- Levels: +0.00, +4.50, +9.00, roof eaves +13.50 m.
- Three occupied floors plus retained timber sawtooth roof.
- Red-brick load-bearing perimeter, steel beams and cast-iron columns at internal grid intersections.
- One tall arched façade opening per structural bay.
- West stair stays inside bay 1 / A–B. East stair stays inside bay 6 / D–E.

## Intervention lock
- Atrium: east–west bays 3–4 × north–south bays B–C = exactly 18.0 × 12.0 m.
- Remove floor plates through levels 1 and 2 inside that rectangle.
- Remove only the single internal cast-iron column at grid 4/C.
- Keep all columns on the atrium perimeter.
- Remove roof only above the same 18 × 12 m rectangle.
- Add independent dark-bronze steel frame on atrium perimeter.
- Add glazed roof lantern, set back 1.5 m from atrium edges.
- Add two glass lifts along the south atrium edge.
- Keep both end stairs and add one service riser beside each.

## Hotel program
- 42 keys total.
- Ground: 2 accessible rooms west/north; west/south entrance and reception; atrium lounge/bar; east/north restaurant and kitchen; east/south back of house and staff.
- Level 1: 20 rooms along north/south façades, 2.0 m gallery around atrium, housekeeping beside risers.
- Level 2: 20 keys including one suite at each end, same gallery logic.
- No guest room, WC or service room occupies the atrium void.

## Graphic code
- KEEP: charcoal.
- REMOVE: coral red, dashed/hatched.

## Validation checklist
- Six long bays, four short bays.
- Atrium 18 × 12 m at 3–4 / B–C.
- Only column 4/C removed.
- Stairs at 1/A–B and 6/D–E.
- Levels stay 4.50 m apart.
- Sawtooth roof remains outside atrium.
- New frame never bears on an unsupported brick pier.
- Window rhythm is identical in plan, section, façade and axonometry.

## Safety
Concept study only. Site survey, hazardous-material investigation and licensed architectural, structural, fire, accessibility, MEP and conservation review are required before construction.
